Identity Verification

In order to be able to trade or deposit/withdraw money to/from your account, you need to verify your identity and address with official documents.

Or if you are a business, you can verify your business information by clicking on the button below

Profile / KYC

Review your personal account details and complete Know Your Customer verification to access all BRICS PAY features.

Keep your legal name and contact information up to date. Identity documents can be submitted securely through the Document Upload section.

Business / KYB

Verify your organization with Know Your Business checks to enable business payments and related services.

Prepare your registration details and authorized representative information before starting business verification.

Proof Of Residence

Provide a recent document that confirms your residential address. Accepted documents may include a utility bill, bank statement, or official government correspondence dated within the last three months.

Make sure your full name, address, and issue date are clearly visible. Use Document Upload to submit your proof securely.

Document Upload

Upload a clear image or PDF of your verification document. Files must be 10 MiB or smaller.

Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, or PDF. Maximum file size: 10 MiB.

API Access

Manage integrations with BRICS PAY APIs. Keep any API credentials private and use a secure server-side environment for production requests.

API access and credentials are available to eligible verified accounts. Contact support to request access or review integration guidance.
